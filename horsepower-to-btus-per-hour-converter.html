How to convert horsepower to btu per hour

Formula: BTU per Hour = Horsepower × 2544.4336.

Factors follow NIST SP 811. The mechanical horsepower used here is exactly 550 ft·lbf/s = 745.69987158227022 W; the metric horsepower (PS, 735.5 W) is a different unit.

Worked example

Question: How many btu per hour is 150 hp — a family car's engine?

Steps: Take the value in horsepower: 150 hp. Apply the formula above. That gives 381665 BTU/h.

Result: 150 hp = 381665 BTU/h.

Good to know about this pair

BTU per Hour: US air conditioners and furnaces are rated in BTU/h; divide by 3,412 to get kW, roughly.

Horsepower: One mechanical horsepower is exactly 550 ft·lbf/s (745.69987 W). Metric PS (735.5 W) and boiler hp are different units.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert horsepower to btu per hour?

Multiply the value in horsepower by 2544.4336: hp × 2544.4336 = BTU/h. For example, 150 hp × 2544.4336 = 381665 BTU/h. The converter above does this instantly.

How many btu per hour are in 1 horsepower?

Exactly 2544.4336 BTU/h (shown to eight significant figures). The full relationship: 1 hp = 2544.4336 BTU/h, and 1 BTU/h = 0.000393 hp.

How many btu per hour is 150 hp?

150 hp = 381665 BTU/h — that is a family car's engine, a handy reference for this pair.

How do I convert btu per hour back to horsepower?

Divide by 2544.4336 — or use the BTU per Hour to Horsepower converter, which does it instantly.
