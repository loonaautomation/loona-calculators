🔢 Numerology Calculator

Your birth date and your name hold a small set of core numbers that numerology has used for centuries to describe character, talents and life direction. Enter yours below — this calculator shows every number AND every step of how it was worked out, so nothing is a black box.

Quick answer: Numbers come from two sources — your birth date (Life Path and Personal Year) and the letters of your full birth name (Expression, Soul Urge and Personality), using the standard Pythagorean chart where A=1, B=2 … I=9 and the cycle then repeats. Totals are reduced by adding their digits, but the master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are kept, never reduced.

Names are calculated with the standard A–Z Pythagorean chart, so enter your name in Latin letters (A–Z), exactly as it appears on your birth certificate.

How to use the Numerology Calculator

  1. Enter your full birth name (Latin letters, as on your birth certificate) and your date of birth.
  2. Choose the year for your Personal Year number — the current year is filled in.
  3. Read your five core numbers, then open “How it was worked out” under any result to see every addition and reduction step.
  4. For compatibility, enter a second birth date in the compatibility box.
Example: Ada Lovelace, born 10 December 1815 — Life Path: month 12 → 3, day 10 → 1, year 1815 → 6, total 3 + 1 + 6 = 10 → 1. Her name gives Expression 9, Soul Urge 1 and Personality 8. The full step-by-step working is shown further down this page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which name should I enter?

Your full name as it appears on your birth certificate — that is the name standard numerology uses for the Expression, Soul Urge and Personality numbers. A married or changed name adds a secondary vibration, but the birth name is always the foundation.

What are master numbers?

11, 22 and 33 are called master numbers. When a total lands on one of them, the standard method does not reduce it any further, because it is read as a higher-voltage version of its root (11 of 2, 22 of 4, 33 of 6). This calculator keeps master numbers and tells you when one appears in your working.

Why does the letter Y sometimes count as a vowel?

In the standard method, Y works as a vowel when it is the only vowel sound in its syllable and is not sitting next to another vowel (as in Lynn or Mary); next to a vowel it works as a consonant (as in Young). This calculator applies that rule and shows exactly which letters it counted as vowels in your working.

Is numerology scientifically proven?

No — numerology is a traditional interpretive system, not a science. Treat the results as entertainment and food for reflection, never as a basis for medical, financial or legal decisions.

How it works — the standard method

Numerology in the Pythagorean tradition turns letters into numbers with a fixed chart — the letters simply cycle through 1 to 9:

The Pythagorean letter chart

1A · J · S
2B · K · T
3C · L · U
4D · M · V
5E · N · W
6F · O · X
7G · P · Y
8H · Q · Z
9I · R

Life Path — reduce the month, the day and the year of birth separately (adding their digits until one digit — or a master number 11, 22 or 33 — remains), add the three results, and reduce once more the same way. Expression (Destiny) — add the values of every letter of the full birth name and reduce. Soul Urge — the same, using only the vowels (Y counts as a vowel when it is the only vowel sound in its syllable and is not next to another vowel). Personality — the same, using only the consonants. Personal Year — add the reduced birth month, reduced birth day and reduced chosen year, then reduce to a single digit 1–9; it runs in repeating 9-year cycles.

Worked example — Ada Lovelace, born 10 December 1815

Life Path: Month 12 → 1 + 2 = 3. Day 10 → 1 + 0 = 1. Year 1815 → 1 + 8 + 1 + 5 = 15 → 1 + 5 = 6. Total 3 + 1 + 6 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1.

Expression: ADA = 1 + 4 + 1 = 6. LOVELACE = 3 + 6 + 4 + 5 + 3 + 1 + 3 + 5 = 30. Full-name total 6 + 30 = 36 → 3 + 6 = 9.

Soul Urge (vowels): A + A + O + E + A + E = 1 + 1 + 6 + 5 + 1 + 5 = 19 → 10 → 1.

Personality (consonants): D + L + V + L + C = 4 + 3 + 4 + 3 + 3 = 17 → 1 + 7 = 8.
