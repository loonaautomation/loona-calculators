🏋️ One-Rep Max Calculator

Enter the weight you lifted and how many reps you completed, and estimate your one-rep maximum with three standard formulas — then see working weights at common training percentages of that max.

Quick answer: Epley: 1RM = weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). Lifting 100 kg for 5 reps gives 100 × (1 + 5/30) = 116.7 kg. Estimates are most reliable for sets of 1–10 reps.

How to use the One-Rep Max Calculator

  1. Enter the weight you lifted (any unit — results use the same unit).
  2. Enter the reps completed with good form (1–15 sensible).
  3. Compare the three formula estimates and the percentage table for training loads.
Example: 100 kg × 5 reps: Epley 116.7 kg, Brzycki 112.5 kg, Lombardi 117.5 kg — a consensus around 112–118 kg. At 80% of the Epley max, the working weight is 93.3 kg.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which formula is most accurate?

All three agree closely for low rep counts and drift apart above 10 reps. Brzycki is popular for general training, Epley is the most widely used, and averaging them is a sensible approach. None replaces an actual, safely tested max.

Why not just test my true 1RM?

A true maximal attempt carries injury risk and needs spotters and experience. Estimating from a submaximal set is the safer everyday method used by most programs.

Do the percentages program my training?

They are the standard guide: about 95% ≈ 2 reps, 90% ≈ 4, 85% ≈ 6, 80% ≈ 8, 75% ≈ 10, 70% ≈ 12 for many lifters — individual variation is real, so treat them as starting points.

Is this medical advice?

No — a training math estimate. Lift within your ability, use proper form and supervision for heavy attempts.

How it works — the formula

Epley: 1RM = w × (1 + r ÷ 30). Brzycki: 1RM = w × 36 ÷ (37 − r). Lombardi: 1RM = w × r^0.10. All three are empirical fits to strength data; they coincide at r = 1 (the weight itself).

Worked example

Inputs: w = 100, r = 5.

Steps: Epley: 100 × (1 + 5÷30) = 100 × 1.16667 = 116.7. Brzycki: 100 × 36 ÷ 32 = 112.5. Lombardi: 100 × 5^0.10 = 100 × 1.1746 = 117.5. 80% of Epley max = 0.8 × 116.7 = 93.3.

Result: Estimated 1RM ≈ 112.5–117.5 across the three formulas.
