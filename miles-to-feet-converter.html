How to convert miles to feet

Formula: Feet = Miles × 5280.

Factors follow the BIPM SI Brochure and NIST SP 811. The inch is defined as exactly 25.4 mm under the International Yard and Pound Agreement of 1959, so every imperial–metric length factor on this page is exact.

Good to know about this pair

Foot: The foot is exactly 0.3048 m; heights in the US are usually given in feet and inches together.

Mile: The statute mile is exactly 1,609.344 m. Do not confuse it with the nautical mile (exactly 1,852 m), used at sea and in aviation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert miles to feet?

Multiply the value in miles by 5280: mi × 5280 = ft. For example, 26.2 mi × 5280 = 138336 ft. The converter above does this instantly.

How many feet are in 1 mile?

Exactly 5280 ft (shown to eight significant figures). The full relationship: 1 mi = 5280 ft, and 1 ft = 0.000189 mi.

How many feet is 26.2 mi?

26.2 mi = 138336 ft — that is a marathon, in miles, a handy reference for this pair.

How do I convert feet back to miles?

Divide by 5280 — or use the Foot to Mile converter, which does it instantly.

What should I watch out for when converting miles to feet?

The foot is exactly 0.3048 m; heights in the US are usually given in feet and inches together. The statute mile is exactly 1,609.344 m. Do not confuse it with the nautical mile (exactly 1,852 m), used at sea and in aviation.
