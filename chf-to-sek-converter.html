A classic safe-haven currency. Swiss cash payments round to the nearest 5 rappen because 1 and 2 rappen coins no longer circulate.

Sweden is among the world's most cashless economies; öre coins were withdrawn, and cash prices round to the nearest krona.

The CHF is the currency of Switzerland and Liechtenstein; its minor unit is the rappen (centime). The SEK is the currency of Sweden; its minor unit is the öre. Reference rates are published by the European Central Bank for statistical comparison — they are mid-market benchmarks, and the rate you are offered by a bank or bureau will include their margin.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CHF to SEK reference rate?

The European Central Bank reference rate for 06 November 2026 gives 1 CHF = 12.059499 SEK. It is a once-a-day reference rate, not a price you can deal at.

How much is 100 CHF in SEK?

100 CHF = 1205.95 SEK at the 06 November 2026 reference rate (12.059499 SEK per CHF). Amounts at other values are in the table on this page.

What is the inverse rate, SEK to CHF?

1 SEK = 0.082922 CHF at the same reference rate. Use the SEK to CHF converter for that direction.

Why is the rate at my bank or exchange shop different?

The ECB reference rate is a mid-market benchmark published for comparison. Banks, card networks and exchange shops add a margin (often 1–5%) plus fees, so the amount you actually receive is lower. Compare the margin, not just the headline rate.

How often does this rate change?

The ECB publishes reference rates once per business day (around 16:00 CET). The rate on this page is the snapshot of 06 November 2026; treat it as indicative and check a live source before a large exchange.
