    How to use the Simple Calculator

    1. Tap the number buttons (or type on your keyboard) to enter the first number.
    2. Tap + − × or ÷, then enter the second number. You can keep chaining — 2 + 3 + 4 works out each step as you go.
    3. Press = to see the answer. Press = again to repeat the last operation, tap Copy result to copy the answer, or tap any line on the history tape to reuse an earlier answer.
    4. Made a mistake? ⌫ deletes the last digit, C clears just the number you are typing, and AC clears everything.
    Example: A shop bill comes to 1,250 and you add a 175 delivery fee, then split it between 2 people: type 1250 + 175 = and the tape shows 1,250 + 175 = 1,425. Then type ÷ 2 = for 712.5 each.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What does the % button do?

    It divides the number before it by 100, so 50% becomes 0.5 and 10% becomes 0.1. For example, 200 × 10% = 20 — handy for quick discounts, tips and tax. For percentage change between two numbers, use our Percentage Calculator.

    How does the history tape work?

    Every time you press =, the finished sum is added to the tape under the calculator, like the paper roll on an old adding machine. Tap any line on the tape to bring its answer back and keep calculating with it, and use Clear history to empty the tape. The tape lives only in this page in your browser — nothing is sent to or stored on a server.

    Can I use my keyboard?

    Yes. The number keys and . type digits, + − * / choose an operation, Enter or = finishes the sum, % is percent, Backspace deletes the last digit, Delete clears the current entry and Escape clears everything.

    What is a simple calculator?

    A simple calculator — also called a basic or four-function calculator — does the four everyday operations: addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, usually with a percent key. It is the calculator most people actually need most of the time: adding up a shopping bill, splitting a restaurant cheque, checking homework, working out change or totalling a day’s expenses. This one adds the things people miss on a phone calculator: big buttons that are easy to tap, a history tape so you can check every step, and a proper keyboard layout on desktop.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    • 0–9 and . — type a number
    • + − * / — add, subtract, multiply, divide
    • Enter or = — finish the sum (press again to repeat it)
    • % — percent (divides the number by 100)
    • Backspace — delete the last digit  ·  Delete — clear the current entry (C)  ·  Escape — clear everything (AC)

    Simple or scientific — which do you need?

    If your sum only uses + − × ÷ and %, this Simple Calculator is the faster, friendlier choice — bigger buttons, nothing you do not need, and a tape that shows your working. When a sum needs trigonometry, logarithms, powers, roots or factorials, switch to our Scientific Calculator instead. For sums about percentages themselves — what percent one number is of another, or percentage increase and decrease — the Percentage Calculator explains each step.

    Frequently asked questions

    What happens if I divide by zero?

    The calculator shows “Cannot divide by zero” instead of an answer, because dividing by zero has no defined result in maths. Nothing breaks — press C or AC, or simply start typing a new number, to carry on.

    Should I use the Simple Calculator or the Scientific Calculator?

    Use this Simple Calculator for everyday adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing — shopping, bills, homework and quick totals — where big buttons and a history tape help most. Use the Scientific Calculator when you need sin, cos, tan, logarithms, powers, roots or factorials.
