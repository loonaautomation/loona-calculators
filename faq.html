FAQ

Short, honest answers about cost, accuracy, privacy and languages.

Quick answers

Are Loona Calculators free?

Yes. All 197 calculators are completely free to use, with no sign-up, no subscription and no limit on how many calculations you make.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no accounts on Loona Calculators. Open a calculator, enter your values and read the result.

Are the results accurate?

Each calculator uses the standard formula for its job and is tested before publishing. Results are still estimates: rounding, the values you enter, and rates or rules that change over time can affect them, so verify important results independently before making a decision.

What happens to the numbers I enter?

Nothing leaves your device. Every calculator runs entirely in your web browser, so the values you enter are never sent to us or stored by us.

What kinds of calculators are here?

There are 197 calculators covering everyday maths, age and dates, finance and loans, salary and tax, health and fitness, study grades such as CGPA and GPA, unit conversions, time, and more.

Which languages can I use?

Loona Calculators is published in 40 language versions — 35 languages plus 5 regional variants. Use the language links at the bottom of the homepage to switch.

Who built Loona Calculators?

Loona Calculators is part of Loona Automation, built and maintained by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer from Jaranwala, Pakistan.

How do I report a wrong result?

Use the Feedback page or email loonaautomation@gmail.com. Please include the calculator name, the values you entered and the result you expected — that is exactly what we need to reproduce and fix an error.
