⭐ EuroMillions Number Generator

Generate EuroMillions lucky numbers in one tap: 5 main numbers from 1–50 plus 2 Lucky Stars from 1–12, drawn with secure randomness. Generate up to 5 lines at once for a full playslip.

Quick answer: A EuroMillions line is 5 different main numbers from 1 to 50 plus 2 different Lucky Stars from 1 to 12. There are 139,838,160 possible combinations, so every line has the same 1 in 139,838,160 jackpot chance.

Playing a different game? Use the general Lottery Number Generator for Powerball, Mega Millions, UK Lotto and custom games.

How to use the EuroMillions Number Generator

  1. Choose how many lines you want (1–5).
  2. Press Generate.
  3. Each line shows 5 main numbers and 2 Lucky Stars, sorted like a real ticket.
  4. Press again for a completely new set of lines.
Example: One generated line might be 7 · 15 · 23 · 34 · 48 with Lucky Stars 3 · 9. Any other valid combination — including 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 with stars 1 · 2 — is exactly as likely.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is EuroMillions different from other lotteries?

EuroMillions uses two pools: 5 main numbers from 50 and 2 Lucky Stars from 12. The general Lottery Number Generator on this site covers many games with presets; this page is dedicated to EuroMillions only, including multi-line slips.

What are the odds of winning the EuroMillions jackpot?

1 in 139,838,160 per line: C(50, 5) = 2,118,760 main combinations multiplied by C(12, 2) = 66 star combinations.

Can the same number appear as a main number and a Lucky Star?

Yes. The stars are drawn from a separate pool, so a star can repeat a main number. Within the main numbers themselves — and within the two stars — numbers never repeat.

Do generated numbers improve my chances?

No. Every valid combination has exactly the same probability. A generator is simply a fair, fast way to choose. Play responsibly and never spend money you cannot afford to lose.

How it works — the formula

Each line draws without replacement from each pool using secure randomness (crypto.getRandomValues with rejection sampling), so every combination is equally likely. The number of possible lines is C(50, 5) × C(12, 2), where C(n, k) = n! ÷ (k! × (n−k)!).

This page is a dedicated EuroMillions variant. The general Lottery Number Generator covers EuroMillions as one preset among several games; that page is unchanged.

Worked example

Steps: C(50, 5) = 50! ÷ (5! × 45!) = 2,118,760. C(12, 2) = 12! ÷ (2! × 10!) = 66. Total combinations = 2,118,760 × 66 = 139,838,160.

Result: Jackpot odds of 1 in 139,838,160 per line — the official EuroMillions figure.
