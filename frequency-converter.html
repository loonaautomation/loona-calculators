Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert frequency units accurately?

Pick your pair from the table on this page. Every pair page carries the exact factor or formula for that pair. The hertz is exactly one cycle per second (SI). Revolutions per minute convert exactly: 1 rpm = 1/60 Hz.

Why does each pair have its own page?

Because the trap differs by pair — a US gallon is not an imperial gallon, a troy ounce is not an avoirdupois ounce, and temperature needs an offset, not a factor. Each pair page states the fact that matters for that pair.

Are these frequency conversion factors exact?

Where the unit is defined exactly (the inch at 25.4 mm, the pound at 0.45359237 kg), the factor is exact. Measured units are given to eight significant figures. Factors checked 9 November 2026.
