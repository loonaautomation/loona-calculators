The Karachi – Sydney time difference

Sydney is 6 hours ahead of Karachi in January and 5 hours ahead of Karachi in July, because daylight saving does not start and end on the same dates in both places (and one may not observe it at all).

Worked example

Question: A call is set for 09:00 in Karachi in January. When is that in Sydney?
