📅 Age Calculator

Apni date of birth likhen aur apni bilkul sahi umar maloom karen — saal, mahine aur din ke sath. Forms, naukri applications aur school admission ke liye umar ka sahi hisab bohat kaam ata hai.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Apni date of birth select karen.
  2. Doosri date aaj ki pehle se lagi hai — chaaho to badal sakte ho (masalan kisi khaas date par umar).
  3. Calculate dabayein — poori tafseel neeche aa jaye gi.
Example: Agar aap 15 January 2000 ko paida huay aur aaj 28 October 2026 hai, to aap ki umar 26 saal, 9 mahine, 13 din hai — kul 9,778 din.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the age calculator free?

Yes — 100% free, no signup, no limit. Your dates never leave your device; the calculation happens in your browser.

Can I calculate my age on a specific date?

Yes. Change the second date (Age at Date) to any past or future date, for example an exam or job application deadline.

Does it count leap years?

Yes — the calendar calculation handles leap years automatically.
