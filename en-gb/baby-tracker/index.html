🍼 Baby Feeding, Sleep & Diaper Tracker

Tap a button each time your baby feeds, sleeps or needs a change. The tracker keeps a private log on this device with daily and weekly totals — and prints a clean summary for your pediatrician. No sign-up, no app to install.

Quick answer: One tap logs a feed (breast, bottle or solids), a nap or a diaper change, with the time saved automatically. You can edit or delete any entry, see today’s totals and the last 7 days at a glance, and print a one-page summary for your baby’s doctor.
🔒 Private by design: everything you log is stored only in this browser, on this device. Your baby’s data is never uploaded — Loona has no server for it and never sees it. There is no account and no cloud copy.

How to use the Baby Tracker

  1. (Optional) Add your baby’s name and date of birth — the tracker shows their age and prints it on the summary.
  2. Tap Breast feed, Bottle or Solids when a feed starts, check the time, add a side, an amount or a note if you like, and save.
  3. Tap Start sleep when your baby falls asleep and the same button again at wake-up — or log a diaper change with a single tap.
  4. Watch the Today panel for feeds, total sleep and diaper counts, and the Last 7 days table for the pattern your pediatrician will ask about.
  5. Tap any entry in the log to edit its time or details, or delete it. Everything updates instantly and is saved on the device.
Example: a day with 3 feeds, 2 naps totalling 2 h 30 min and 4 diaper changes shows exactly those totals in the Today panel — and the same numbers appear in the printable summary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my baby’s data private?

Yes. Everything is stored only in this browser on this device (local storage). Loona never receives, uploads or sees your log. There is no account and no cloud copy. If you clear your browser data, the log is erased — export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Does the tracker work offline?

Yes. After the first visit, the page works without an internet connection. Your entries are saved on the device and are still there when you come back — made for one-handed logging at 3 a.m.

How do I show the log to our pediatrician?

Tap “Print summary for the pediatrician”. It builds a clean one-page summary with your baby’s name and age, today’s entries and the last 7 days of feeds, sleep and diapers, ready to print or save as a PDF.

Can I move my log to a new phone?

Yes. Use Export backup on the old phone to save a small file, then Import backup on the new phone. The file moves only where you send it — nothing passes through a Loona server, because there isn’t one.

Can I track twins or two children?

The tracker keeps one baby’s log per browser. For a second baby, use a different browser or a second device profile, and put each baby’s name in the profile box so printouts stay clear.

How it works — your log, on your device

Every entry is a small record — a type (feed, sleep or diaper), a time, and optional details such as the side, a bottle amount in ml, or a note. The records live in your browser’s local storage, the same private space your browser keeps for a site’s own settings. Nothing is sent anywhere: the page does all counting locally, in your browser, each time you open it.

How the totals are counted: an entry belongs to the calendar day on which it starts. A day’s sleep total adds up the finished naps and night sleeps that started that day; a sleep still in progress is shown separately until wake-up is logged. Bottle amounts are totalled in millilitres (fluid ounces are converted at 1 fl oz = 29.5735 ml). A “wet + dirty” change counts as one diaper, one wet and one dirty in the breakdown.

Worked example

Logged: bottle 120 ml at 07:10, breast feed at 10:05, nap 11:00–12:30, wet diaper at 12:45, dirty diaper at 15:20, nap 16:00–17:00, solids at 18:05, wet + dirty diaper at 19:30.

Today panel: Feeds 3 (bottle total 120 ml) · Sleep 2 h 30 min over 2 naps · Diapers 3 (2 wet · 2 dirty in the breakdown).

Please note: this tracker is a memory aid for parents, not a medical device or medical advice. Feeding and sleep needs vary widely between babies — always follow your own pediatrician’s guidance about your baby’s health.
