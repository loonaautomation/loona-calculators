How to convert celsius to fahrenheit

Formula: Fahrenheit = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9, divide by 5, then add 32.

Worked example

Question: How many fahrenheit is 37 °C — normal human body temperature?

Steps: Take the value in celsius: 37 °C. Apply the formula: Fahrenheit = (°C × 9/5) + 32. That gives 37 → 98.6 °F (rounded to four decimal places).

Result: 37 °C = 98.6 °F.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert celsius to fahrenheit?

Use the formula °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. For example, 20 °C × 9/5 = 36, plus 32 = 68 °F. The converter above applies it instantly.

How many fahrenheit are in 1 degree Celsius?

There is no fixed number — the scales start at different zeros. Each 1 °C step equals a 1.8 °F step, and the anchor points are 0 °C = 32 °F and 100 °C = 212 °F.

What is 40 °C in Fahrenheit?

40 °C = 104 °F — a hot day, and a high fever if it is a body temperature.

How do I convert fahrenheit back to celsius?

Use the reverse conversion — our fahrenheit to celsius converter does it instantly.

At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit equal?

At −40. −40 °C and −40 °F are the same temperature — the only point where the two scales meet.
