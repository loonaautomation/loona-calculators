🍳 Oven Temperature: Gas Mark 5

Gas Mark 5 = 190 °C conventional = 170 °C fan = 375 °F. Moderately hot — pies with crisp bases, roast chicken, puff pastry. The full oven ladder, a worked recipe conversion and the fan rule are below.

Conventional oven190 °C
Fan oven170 °C (conventional − 20 °C)
Fahrenheit375 °F

Worked example

Question: Your British recipe says 'Gas Mark 5' but your oven dial is in Celsius.

Steps: Find the setting on the ladder below: it sits at 190 °C conventional. For a fan oven subtract 20 °C → 170 °C.

Result: Set a conventional oven to 190 °C, or a fan oven to 170 °C (equivalent to 375 °F).

Moderately hot — pies with crisp bases, roast chicken, puff pastry.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Gas Mark 5 in fan oven temperature?

170 °C fan. Fan ovens cook more efficiently, so the standard rule is conventional temperature minus 20 °C.

What is Gas Mark 5 in Fahrenheit?

375 °F. The full ladder is in the table on this page.

What is Gas Mark 5 in Gas Mark?

Gas Mark 5

What can I cook at Gas Mark 5?

Moderately hot — pies with crisp bases, roast chicken, puff pastry.

Why do recipes disagree about oven temperatures?

Because three scales (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Gas Mark) and two oven types (conventional, fan) are in circulation, and older Gas Mark recipes predate fan ovens. Convert the scale first, then subtract 20 °C if your oven is fan-assisted.
