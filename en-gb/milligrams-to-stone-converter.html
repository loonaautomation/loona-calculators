How to convert milligrams to stone

Formula: Stone = Milligrams ÷ 6350293.2.

Factors follow NIST SP 811. The avoirdupois pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kg (1959 agreement), so all pound/ounce/stone factors here are exact.
