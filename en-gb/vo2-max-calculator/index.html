🫁 VO2 Max Calculator

Estimate your VO2 max — the classic measure of aerobic fitness — from either the Cooper 12-minute run (distance covered) or the Rockport one-mile walk test (time, heart rate, weight, age and sex).

Quick answer: Cooper test: VO2 max ≈ (distance in metres − 504.9) ÷ 44.73. Running 2,800 m in 12 minutes gives (2,800 − 504.9) ÷ 44.73 ≈ 51.3 ml/kg/min — excellent for most adults.

How to use the VO2 Max Calculator

  1. Choose your test: Cooper run or Rockport walk.
  2. For Cooper, enter the distance you covered in exactly 12 minutes of running.
  3. For Rockport, enter your one-mile walk time, heart rate at the end, weight, age and sex.
Example: Cooper: 2,800 m in 12 minutes → VO2 max ≈ 51.3. Rockport example: a 40-year-old man, 80 kg, walks a mile in 14:00 with end heart rate 120 → VO2 max ≈ 45.6 (worked below).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good VO2 max?

It depends strongly on age and sex. As a rough guide for adults: below about 30 is low, 35–45 is average-to-good for many age groups, 45–55 is very good, and elite endurance athletes exceed 60–75. Age-graded tables give the fairest reading.

How accurate are these field tests?

The Cooper and Rockport equations were validated against laboratory gas-analysis tests and are typically within about ±3–5 ml/kg/min. They are estimates for training guidance, not medical measurements.

Which test should I choose?

Runners usually pick Cooper (a maximal 12-minute effort — warm up properly and do not attempt it if you are unwell or new to hard exercise). Walkers and beginners pick Rockport, which is submaximal and gentler.

Is this medical advice?

No — an educational fitness estimate. Maximal exercise tests carry risk for some people; check with a doctor first if you have any medical condition or symptoms.

How it works — the formula

Cooper: VO2 max = (distance in m − 504.9) ÷ 44.73. Rockport: VO2 max = 132.853 − 0.0769 × weight(lb) − 0.3877 × age + 6.315 × sex (1 male, 0 female) − 3.2649 × time(min) − 0.1565 × end heart rate. Both give ml/kg/min.

Worked example

Rockport inputs: man, 40 years, 80 kg (= 176.37 lb), 14.00 min mile, end HR 120.

Steps: 132.853 − 0.0769×176.37 − 0.3877×40 + 6.315 − 3.2649×14 − 0.1565×120 = 132.853 − 13.563 − 15.508 + 6.315 − 45.709 − 18.780 = 45.61.

Result: VO2 max ≈ 45.6 ml/kg/min. (The page example above round-states ≈46.) Cooper check: (2,800 − 504.9) ÷ 44.73 = 2,295.1 ÷ 44.73 = 51.3.

Health disclaimer: Educational estimate only — not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified health professional about your own or your child’s health.
