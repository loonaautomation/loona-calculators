Frequently Asked Questions

Where do these exchange rates come from?

They are the European Central Bank's daily euro foreign exchange reference rates of 06 November 2026, cross-calculated for pairs that do not include the euro. The ECB publishes them once per business day for reference only.

Why is the rate here different from the one my bank offers?

The ECB rate is a mid-market benchmark with no margin. Banks and bureaux add their margin and fees on top, so you receive less than the reference value — the difference is their price for the service.

How many currency pairs are listed?

20 widely used currencies, every ordered pair — 380 converter pages, each with its own rate, inverse, amount table and pair notes.
