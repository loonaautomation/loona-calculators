📉 Slope Calculator

Enter two points (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂) to get everything about the line between them: slope, distance, midpoint, angle and the equation y = mx + b — with each formula's substitution shown.

Quick answer: Slope = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). For (1, 2) and (4, 8): slope = 6 ÷ 3 = 2, distance = √(3² + 6²) = √45 ≈ 6.71, and the midpoint is (2.5, 5).

How to use the Slope Calculator

  1. Enter the coordinates of the first point.
  2. Enter the coordinates of the second point.
  3. Read the slope, distance, midpoint and the line's equation.
Example: Points (1, 2) and (4, 8): slope 2, distance √45 ≈ 6.708, midpoint (2.5, 5), and the line equation y = 2x + 0 — it passes through the origin, which you can check: at x = 1, y = 2 ✓.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a slope of 2 mean?

For every 1 unit you move right, the line rises 2 units. A slope of 0 is flat, a negative slope falls to the right, and an undefined slope (division by zero) is a vertical line.

How is distance between two points calculated?

With Pythagoras: distance = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²). The coordinate differences are the two legs of a right triangle and the distance is its hypotenuse.

What is the midpoint formula?

Average the coordinates: midpoint = ((x₁ + x₂) ÷ 2, (y₁ + y₂) ÷ 2). It is the point exactly halfway along the segment.

What if both x values are the same?

The line is vertical: slope is undefined and there is no y = mx + b equation. The calculator reports the vertical line x = that value, plus the distance and midpoint as normal.

How it works — the formula

Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Distance: d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²). Midpoint: ((x₁ + x₂) ÷ 2, (y₁ + y₂) ÷ 2). Line equation: using point 1, b = y₁ − m·x₁, giving y = mx + b. Angle: θ = arctan(m), in degrees from the positive x-axis.

Worked example

Inputs: (1, 2) and (4, 8).

Steps: Slope = (8 − 2) ÷ (4 − 1) = 6 ÷ 3 = 2. Distance = √((4−1)² + (8−2)²) = √(9 + 36) = √45 = 6.708. Midpoint = ((1+4)÷2, (2+8)÷2) = (2.5, 5). Intercept: b = 2 − 2×1 = 0, so the line is y = 2x.

Result: Slope 2, distance 6.708, midpoint (2.5, 5), equation y = 2x.
