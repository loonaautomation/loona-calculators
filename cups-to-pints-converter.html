How to convert cups (us) to pints (us liquid)

Formula: Pints (US liquid) = Cups (US) × 0.5.

US liquid measures follow NIST SP 811: the US gallon is exactly 231 cubic inches (3.785411784 L). The imperial gallon (4.54609 L) is a different unit and is not used on these pages.

Worked example

Question: How many pints (us liquid) is 2 cup — flour for a small batch of pancakes, roughly?

Steps: Take the value in cups (us): 2 cup. Apply the formula above. That gives 1 pt.

Result: 2 cup = 1 pt.

Good to know about this pair

Pint (US liquid): A US pint is exactly 16 fl oz — 'a pint's a pound the world around' — but a UK pint is 568 mL, about 20% larger.

Cup (US): A US customary cup is exactly 8 fluid ounces (236.588 mL); a metric cup is 250 mL, so the same recipe differs by country.

Multiply the value in cups (us) by 0.5: cup × 0.5 = pt. For example, 2 cup × 0.5 = 1 pt. The converter above does this instantly.

Exactly 0.5 pt (shown to eight significant figures). The full relationship: 1 cup = 0.5 pt, and 1 pt = 2 cup.
