📅 Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth and find your exact age — in years, months and days. The correct age matters for forms, job applications and school admissions, and this tool gets it exactly right.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Select your date of birth.
  2. The second date is today — you can change it to find your age on any specific date (for example, an exam deadline).
  3. Press Calculate — the full breakdown appears below.
Example: If you were born on 15 January 2000 and today is 28 October 2026, your age is 26 years, 9 months and 13 days — 9,783 days in total.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the age calculator free?

Yes — 100% free, no signup, no limit. Your dates never leave your device; the calculation happens in your browser.

Can I calculate my age on a specific date?

Yes. Change the second date (Age at Date) to any past or future date, for example an exam or job application deadline.

Does it count leap years?

Yes — the calendar calculation handles leap years automatically.

Loona Calculators › Money & Islamic › Zakat Calculator

🕌 Zakat Calculator

Enter your wealth — cash, bank balance, gold, silver and business goods — and find out exactly how much zakat you owe. Everything is calculated inside your own browser; no data ever leaves your device.

How to use the Zakat Calculator

  1. Fill in the fields that apply to you and leave the rest blank.
  2. Enter today's per-gram rate for gold or silver — the nisab check depends on it.
  3. Press Calculate — the nisab and your zakat amount appear below.
Example: Agar aap ki kul zakatable wealth Rs 10,00,000 hai aur nisab pura hai, to zakat = 2.5% = Rs 25,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is nisab?

Nisab is the minimum wealth above which zakat becomes obligatory: 87.48 grams of gold or 612.36 grams of silver in value. This calculator uses the lower of the two when you enter both rates.

Is my financial data saved?

No. Everything is calculated inside your browser — nothing is sent or stored anywhere.

Should I verify my zakat?

Yes — this tool gives an estimate. For your exact religious obligation, please confirm with a trusted scholar (mufti).

Loona Calculators › Education › CGPA Calculator

🎓 CGPA Calculator

Enter your subject marks and credit hours to get your semester GPA instantly. If you also enter a previous CGPA, the tool combines everything into your cumulative CGPA.

How to use the CGPA Calculator

  1. Enter the marks (out of 100) and credit hours for each subject — up to 8 subjects.
  2. If you know your previous CGPA and its total credit hours, add them too.
  3. Press Calculate.
Example: If a student's grade points across 5 subjects, weighted by their credit hours, average to 3.45, the semester GPA is 3.45.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which grade scale is used?

The common HEC scale: 85+ marks = 4.0, 80 = 3.7, 75 = 3.3, 70 = 3.0, 65 = 2.7, 61 = 2.3, 58 = 2.0, 55 = 1.7, 51 = 1.3, 50 = 1.0, below 50 = 0.

My university uses a different scale. Can I still use it?

The result is an estimate on the HEC scale. Check your university's official scale for exact figures.

How is CGPA different from GPA?

GPA is for one semester; CGPA is the average of all semesters weighted by credit hours.

％ Percentage Calculator

Three everyday percentage calculations in one place: find a percentage of a number, see what percent one number is of another, and measure the percentage increase or decrease between two values.

How to use the Percentage Calculator

  1. First select which calculation you need.
  2. Enter the X and Y values.
  3. Press Calculate.
Example: 20% of 500 = 100. And if a value grows from 400 to 500, that is a 25% increase.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find X% of a number?

Choose the first mode, enter X (the percent) and Y (the number). Example: 20% of 500 = 100.

How is percentage change calculated?

(New − Old) ÷ Old × 100. A positive answer is an increase, negative is a decrease.

Is it free for marks percentage?

Yes — and for marks specifically, try our Marks Percentage Calculator.

🌙 Hijri Date Converter

Convert any Gregorian (English) date into the Islamic Hijri date — or the other way around. Perfect for checking Ramadan and Eid dates, Islamic birthdays and religious occasions.

How to use the Hijri Date Converter

  1. Select the direction — Gregorian to Hijri, or Hijri to Gregorian.
  2. Enter or select the date (for Hijri: the year, month and day).
  3. Press Calculate.
Example: 28 October 2026 corresponds to the Hijri date 18 Jumada al-Ula 1448 AH (Umm al-Qura calendar).

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Hijri calendar is used?

The Umm al-Qura calendar, the official calendar of Saudi Arabia, which most apps and devices use.

Why is my country's date one day different?

Some countries sight the moon locally, so the official date can differ by one day from the calculated calendar.

Can I convert a Hijri birthday to Gregorian?

Yes — choose the Hijri to Gregorian direction and enter the Hijri year, month and day.

Loona Calculators › Money & Finance › EMI & Loan Calculator

🏦 EMI & Loan Calculator

Enter the loan amount, rate and tenure to see your monthly installment (EMI), the total interest or profit, and the total you will repay. For bank, car and personal loans — always worth checking before you sign.

How to use the EMI & Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount (Rs).
  2. Enter the yearly interest or profit rate (%).
  3. Enter the tenure — in months or years — and press Calculate.
Example: A loan of Rs 1,00,000 at 10% per year for 12 months gives a monthly installment of Rs 8,791.59, with total interest of Rs 5,499.08.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay every month until the loan ends — part principal, part interest/profit.

Does this work for Islamic (interest-free) financing?

If your financing gives a total payable amount, enter the profit rate that matches it — the installment math is the same. Ask your bank for the exact rate.

What if the interest rate is 0%?

Enter 0 — the calculator simply divides the amount by the number of months.

⚖️ BMI Calculator

Enter your weight and height to learn whether your weight is in the healthy range — underweight, normal, overweight or obese. Height can be entered in centimetres or in feet and inches.

How to use the BMI Calculator

  1. Enter your weight in kilograms.
  2. Enter your height — in cm, or in feet plus inches.
  3. Press Calculate — your BMI and healthy weight range appear below.
Example: 70 kg wazan aur 175 cm qad par BMI = 22.9 hota hai, jo Normal (sehatmand) range me ata hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For adults, 18.5 to 24.9 is the normal range. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25–29.9 overweight, and 30+ obese.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

BMI is a general guide. Muscular athletes can show a high BMI without excess fat — consult a doctor for a full assessment.

Can children use this?

This calculator uses the adult scale. Children's BMI is read on age-based charts.

📈 Compound Interest

How much does money grow over time? Enter the principal, rate and years to see the final amount with a year-by-year growth table. You can include a monthly deposit to model regular savings.

How to use the Compound Interest

  1. Enter the starting amount (principal).
  2. Enter the yearly rate (%) and the number of years; choose how often interest compounds.
  3. Optionally add a monthly deposit, then press Calculate.
Example: Rs 1,00,000 at 10% compound interest per year becomes Rs 1,61,051 in 5 years — Rs 61,051 of it is pure interest.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is compound interest?

Interest earned on both your money and on the interest already added — so growth speeds up over time.

How often should compounding be?

More frequent compounding (monthly, daily) grows money slightly faster than yearly at the same rate.

Can I add monthly savings?

Yes — enter a monthly deposit and it is included in the growth table.

📝 Marks Percentage

Enter your obtained marks and the total marks to get your percentage and grade instantly — for matric, intermediate, university and every other exam.

How to use the Marks Percentage

  1. Enter the obtained marks.
  2. Enter the total marks.
  3. Press Calculate.
Example: If you score 720 out of 850 total marks, your percentage is 84.71% and the grade is A.

How is percentage calculated from marks?

Which grade scale is shown?

💡 Electricity Bill

Estimate your monthly electricity bill before it arrives. Enter the units you used — every slab rate can be edited, so you can type in the latest rates of your own company (LESCO, IESCO, K-Electric and others) for the closest estimate.

How to use the Electricity Bill

  1. Enter the units used this month (the difference between meter readings).
  2. Check the slab rates — common recent rates are pre-filled and can be replaced with the rates printed on your own bill.
  3. Optionally add GST and other charges (such as FPA), then press Calculate.
Example: For 300 units with the pre-filled rates, the energy cost is Rs 8,697 and, with GST (18%) added, the bill comes to about Rs 10,262 (the actual bill depends on your company's latest rates).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my real bill different?

Rates change with government notifications, fuel price adjustments (FPA) and your consumer category (protected/unprotected). Enter the rates printed on your own bill for the closest estimate.

What is a slab?

Electricity is charged in blocks: the first 100 units at one rate, the next 100 at a higher rate, and so on. This calculator applies each slab correctly.

Does it include FPA and fixed charges?

Add them in the Other charges field — they appear on your bill and vary every month.

🏡 Marla / Kanal Converter

Convert land and plot area between the units used in Pakistan and India: Marla, Kanal, Acre, Gaj (square yards), square feet and square metres — with both marla sizes, 272.25 and 225 sq ft.

How to use the Marla / Kanal Converter

  1. Enter the value (how much area).
  2. Choose which unit it is in (From).
  3. Select the marla size and press Calculate — every unit appears together.
Example: 1 Kanal = 20 Marla = 5,445 sq ft (standard marla 272.25 sq ft par). 1 Acre = 8 Kanal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many square feet are in 1 marla?

Standard marla = 272.25 sq ft. In some areas an older marla of 225 sq ft is used — you can select it in this tool.

How many marla in 1 kanal and 1 acre?

1 kanal = 20 marla. 1 acre = 8 kanal = 160 marla.

What is a gaj?

Gaj is the square yard: 1 gaj = 9 sq ft.

💼 Salary Tax Calculator

Enter your monthly salary to see how much income tax will be deducted and what your net take-home pay will be. Calculated on Pakistan's salaried tax slabs (Tax Year 2026) — or apply your own flat percentage instead.

Is this my exact payroll tax?

It is a close estimate on salary income only. Other income, allowances and exemptions can change your real tax.

What is effective tax rate?

Your total tax divided by your total salary, shown as a percentage.

What will that sale item really cost you? Enter the price and the discount percentage to see the final price and your savings — with options for a second extra discount and tax added afterwards.

Loona Calculators › Education › CGPA → Percentage

🔄 CGPA → Percentage

Convert CGPA into percentage — with the famous ×9.5 formula on the 10-point scale, a direct conversion on the 4-point scale, or your own university's custom multiplier.

How to use the CGPA → Percentage

  1. Enter your CGPA or GPA.
  2. Select the scale: 10, 4 or custom.
  3. Press Calculate.
Example: A CGPA of 8.5 on the 10-point scale × 9.5 = 80.75%. A GPA of 3.6 on the 4-point scale = 90%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why multiply CGPA by 9.5?

The ×9.5 formula is used by CBSE and many boards to convert a 10-point CGPA into an approximate percentage.

Is the converted percentage exact?

It is an estimate — every board and university can use its own formula. For official use, confirm with your institution.

Can I convert percentage back to CGPA?

Divide by the same multiplier: percentage ÷ 9.5 gives the 10-scale CGPA.

Loona Calculators › Health › Pregnancy Due Date

🤰 Pregnancy Due Date

Enter the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP) to find your expected due date, which week of pregnancy you are in, and your current trimester.

How to use the Pregnancy Due Date

  1. Select the first day of your last period.
  2. Press Calculate.
Example: If the LMP is 1 February 2026, the due date is about 8 November 2026 (LMP + 280 days).

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the due date calculated?

By Naegele's rule: LMP + 280 days (40 weeks), assuming a regular 28-day cycle.

Is the due date exact?

Only about 1 in 20 babies arrive exactly on the due date — it is an estimate. Your doctor's ultrasound dates can be more accurate.

What if my cycle is longer or shorter?

Due dates shift a little with cycle length. Confirm with your gynecologist.

Loona Calculators › Money & Finance › Simple Interest

💰 Simple Interest

Simple interest made easy: enter the principal, the yearly rate and the time to see the interest and the total amount — for school maths, loans and everyday money questions.

How to use the Simple Interest

  1. Enter the principal (the original amount).
  2. Enter the rate (%) and the time (years plus months).
  3. Press Calculate.
Example: Rs 50,000 at 8% simple interest per year earns Rs 12,000 in 3 years — a total amount of Rs 62,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the simple interest formula?

I = P × R × T ÷ 100, where P is principal, R is the yearly rate and T is time in years.

How is simple interest different from compound?

Simple interest is charged only on the original principal; compound interest also grows on past interest.

Can I enter months only?

Yes — put 0 years and your months in the months field.

How is attendance percentage calculated?

My college requires 75%. What should I enter?

Keep the target at 75 (it is pre-filled) and enter your held and attended classes.
