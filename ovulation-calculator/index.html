🌸 Ovulation Calculator

Planning a pregnancy? Enter the first day of your last period and your usual cycle length to estimate your ovulation day, your most fertile window and your next period.

How to use the Ovulation Calculator

  1. Select the first day of your last period.
  2. Enter your cycle length in days (28 is pre-filled).
  3. Press Calculate — your estimated ovulation and fertile window appear below.
Example: With a last period starting 1 October 2026 and a 28-day cycle, ovulation is estimated on Thursday, 15 October 2026, the fertile window is 10-15 October, and the next period is expected on 29 October 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is ovulation estimated?

Ovulation usually happens about 14 days before the next period, so it is estimated as: last period + cycle length − 14 days. The fertile window is the 5 days before ovulation plus ovulation day itself.

Can I use this as contraception?

No. Cycles vary from month to month and estimates can be off by several days. Never rely on a calculator to prevent pregnancy.

What is the due date shown?

If you conceive in this cycle, an estimated due date of ovulation + 266 days. It is an estimate only — your doctor will confirm with scans.
