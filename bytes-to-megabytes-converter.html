How to convert bytes to megabytes

Formula: Megabytes = Bytes ÷ 1000000.

Decimal units (KB, MB, GB, TB) follow the SI definition used by storage manufacturers and macOS: 1 KB = 1,000 bytes. Binary units (KiB, MiB) follow IEC 80000-13: 1 KiB = 1,024 bytes — the gap is why a '500 GB' drive shows about 465 GiB in Windows.

Worked example

Question: How many megabytes is 2000 B — a short plain-text email?

Steps: Take the value in bytes: 2000 B. Apply the formula above. That gives 0.002 MB.

Result: 2000 B = 0.002 MB.

Good to know about this pair

Megabyte: A three-minute MP3 song is about 3–5 MB; phone photos are typically 2–8 MB.

Byte: One byte holds roughly one character of plain text; a short email is about 2,000 bytes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert bytes to megabytes?

Multiply the value in bytes by 0.000001: B × 0.000001 = MB. For example, 2000 B × 0.000001 = 0.002 MB. The converter above does this instantly.

How many megabytes are in 1 byte?

Exactly 0.000001 MB (shown to eight significant figures). The full relationship: 1 B = 0.000001 MB, and 1 MB = 1000000 B.

How many megabytes is 2000 B?

2000 B = 0.002 MB — that is a short plain-text email, a handy reference for this pair.

How do I convert megabytes back to bytes?

Divide by 0.000001 — or use the Megabyte to Byte converter, which does it instantly.

What should I watch out for when converting bytes to megabytes?
