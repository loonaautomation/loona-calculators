🌍 Working Days Calculator — Europe

How many real working days are in your period? Pick a European country and two dates — weekends and that country's own public holidays are taken out automatically, using its official holiday calendar.

How to use the Europe working days calculator

  1. Choose one of the 28 countries.
  2. Select the start and end dates of your period.
  3. Press Calculate — you get total days, weekend days, the public holidays found in the range (with names and dates), and the true working days.
Example: Working days in Germany, December 2026: 22 — out of 31 total days, with 8 weekend days and 2 public holidays. Try it above: Germany is pre-selected with the December 2026 range filled in.

Working days in Europe at a glance — 2026

Europe has no single holiday calendar: in 2026 the number of national public holidays runs from 4 in Switzerland (where the cantons set their own) to 16 in Romania, and the same period can hold very different working-day counts in different countries. How it works — the formula

The calculator walks through your range one calendar day at a time, including both the start and the end date. Each day is classified as a weekend day (Saturday or Sunday), a public holiday from the selected country's official list, or a working day. Holiday data covers 2025 to 2027; Easter-based holidays are calculated for each year, with Greece and Romania following the Orthodox calendar.

Formula: Working days = total days in the inclusive range − weekend days − public holidays that fall on a weekday.

Worked example

Inputs: Country: Germany; start date: Tuesday, 1 December 2026; end date: Thursday, 31 December 2026.

Steps: The inclusive range holds 31 days. Weekend days (Saturdays and Sundays): 8. Public holidays inside the range: Christmas Day (Friday, 25 December); Boxing Day (Saturday, 26 December) — 2 in total, of which 1 fall on a weekday. Working days = 31 − 8 − 1 = 22.

Result: Working days in Germany, December 2026: 22 — out of 31 total days, with 8 weekend days and 2 public holidays.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which countries does the Europe working days calculator cover?

Twenty countries: the United Kingdom (England and Wales), Ireland, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Belgium, Austria, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland, Greece, Czechia, Romania, Hungary and Switzerland — each with its own official public holiday list for 2025 to 2027 built in.

How are public holidays handled for each country?

Every country uses its own national list. Easter-based holidays are calculated per year — Greece and Romania follow the Orthodox Easter calendar, the rest the Western one. Regional holidays are not included; each country page explains what is and is not counted.

Why does Switzerland show only 4 holidays?

Swiss public holidays are set by the cantons, not the federal government. Only Swiss National Day (1 August) is federal, and only four days are holidays in all 26 cantons. Each canton adds its own days on top.

What counts as a working day?

A Monday to Friday that is not a national public holiday in the selected country. One special case: Hungary decrees a few working Saturdays each year (three in 2026), and those Saturdays count as working days for Hungary.

Can I count a range that crosses into another year?

Yes. Holiday data covers 2025, 2026 and 2027, and ranges can span months and years freely — December into January, or a whole year at once. Both the start and the end date are included in the count.
