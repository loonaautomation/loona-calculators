When is Christmas Eve?

Christmas Eve, on 24 December, is for many families the true heart of Christmas — in much of Europe and Latin America it, rather than Christmas Day, is when the main meal is eaten and gifts are opened.

Date rule: 24 December every year. In 2026, Christmas Eve falls on Thursday, 24 December 2026.

Facts about Christmas Eve

  • In countries such as Germany, Poland, Sweden and much of Latin America, Christmas Eve is the main celebration, with gifts exchanged on the evening of the 24th.
  • Midnight Mass on Christmas Eve is one of the best-attended church services of the year in Catholic and many other traditions.
  • The tradition of Santa Claus visiting on Christmas Eve night grew from Saint Nicholas, Sinterklaas and Father Christmas traditions merging over centuries.
  • Children in many countries leave out stockings, shoes or plates on Christmas Eve for gifts and treats.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Christmas Eve?

24 December, the day before Christmas Day — the dates table shows the weekday for this year and the next three.

Why do some countries celebrate on Christmas Eve instead of Christmas Day?

Historical and religious custom: in much of continental Europe the festive evening has always been the focus — the Christmas meal and gift-giving happen after dark on the 24th, following older traditions of starting holy days at sunset.

Is Christmas Eve a public holiday?

It varies: it is a full public holiday in some countries (such as Sweden and parts of Germany, where shops close at midday), a partial holiday in others, and a normal (if shortened) working day in the UK and US.

What is Midnight Mass?

The Christmas Mass celebrated late on Christmas Eve, traditionally at midnight, welcoming Christmas Day. Many churches now hold it earlier in the evening.

How is Christmas Eve different from Christmas Day?

Christmas Eve (24th) is the anticipation — in many cultures the main event; Christmas Day (25th) is the public holiday and family day in the English-speaking tradition. This page counts down to the 24th; we also have a Christmas Day countdown.
