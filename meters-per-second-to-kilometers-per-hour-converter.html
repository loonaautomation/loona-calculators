How to convert metres per second to kilometres per hour

Formula: Kilometres per Hour = metres per second × 3.6. One hour has exactly 3,600 seconds and one kilometre exactly 1,000 metres, so the factor is 3,600 ÷ 1,000 = 3.6 exactly.

Worked example

Question: How many kilometres per hour is 10 m/s — a sprinter running at 10 m/s?

Steps: Take the value in metres per second: 10 m/s. Apply the formula: Kilometres per Hour = metres per second × 3.6. That gives 10 → 36 km/h (rounded to four decimal places).

Result: 10 m/s = 36 km/h.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert metres per second to kilometres per hour?

Multiply the value in metres per second by 3.6: m/s × 3.6 = km/h. For example, 10 m/s × 3.6 = 36 km/h. The converter above does this instantly.

How many kilometres per hour are in 1 metre per second?

3.6 km/h (to four decimal places). The full relationship: Kilometres per Hour = metres per second × 3.6.

How many kilometres per hour is 10 m/s?

10 m/s = 36 km/h — a sprinter running at 10 m/s.

How do I convert kilometres per hour back to metres per second?

Use the reverse conversion — our km/h to metres per second converter does it instantly. For a quick check, divide by the same factor instead.

How do I convert m/s to km/h in my head?

Multiply by 3.6 — or multiply by 4 and subtract a tenth of the result: 10 m/s × 4 = 40, minus 4 = 36 km/h.
