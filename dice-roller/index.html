🎲 Dice Roller

Roll up to six virtual dice at once — standard six-sided dice with real pips, or polyhedral dice like the d20 used in role-playing games. Every roll is powered by secure browser randomness and your full roll history and totals are kept on screen.

How to use the Dice Roller

  1. Choose how many dice to roll (1 to 6).
  2. Pick the die type — d6 is the standard board-game die; d20 is the classic role-playing die.
  3. Press Roll the Dice and read the individual results, the total and the running history.
Example: A board game needs two dice: choose 2 dice, d6, and roll. If the dice show 4 and 5, the total is 9 — the same move a physical pair of dice would give you, with the history saved below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the virtual dice fair?

Yes. Each die is rolled with crypto.getRandomValues, your browser's secure random source, using rejection sampling so every face is exactly equally likely — no weighting, no patterns, no memory between rolls.

What is a d20?

A d20 is a twenty-sided die, famous from tabletop role-playing games such as Dungeons & Dragons, where it decides whether attacks and skill checks succeed. This roller also offers d4, d8, d10, d12 and d100 (percentile) dice.

What are the odds of rolling doubles?

With two d6 dice there are 36 equally likely combinations and 6 of them are doubles, so the chance is 6 out of 36 — about 16.7%. A double six specifically is 1 in 36, about 2.8%.

Maths

How it works — the formula

Each die is an independent draw from the secure random generator: roll = 1 + (secure random value mod sides), with rejection sampling so no face is favoured. With several dice, the total is simply the sum of the individual rolls, and the average shown is total ÷ number of dice. The most likely total of two d6 is 7, because more combinations produce it than any other sum.

Worked example

Example: You roll 3d6 and get 2, 5 and 6. Total = 2 + 5 + 6 = 13, average per die = 13 ÷ 3 ≈ 4.33. The long-run average of a single d6 is 3.5, so a total of 13 from three dice is a slightly above-average roll.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most common total with two dice?

Seven. Six combinations add to 7 (1+6, 2+5, 3+4, 4+3, 5+2, 6+1), making it more likely than any other total — about 16.7% of rolls.

Can I roll more than 6 dice?

This roller shows up to 6 dice visually so every die stays readable. For picking many numbers at once, use our Random Number Generator, which can draw up to 10,000 numbers in any range.

Is my roll history saved anywhere?

Only on this page, in your browser, while the tab is open. Nothing is uploaded or stored — refresh the page and the history is cleared.
